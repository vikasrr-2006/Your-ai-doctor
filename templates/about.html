About Your AI Doctor

Revolutionizing healthcare with artificial intelligence

Project Overview

"Your AI Doctor" is a full-stack AI-powered healthcare web application that combines advanced machine learning algorithms with a modern, user-friendly interface to provide preliminary disease prediction and help users find appropriate medical care.

AI-Powered Prediction

Uses Decision Tree and Random Forest machine learning models trained on medical data to predict potential diseases based on selected symptoms.

Disease Information

Provides comprehensive information including treatment options, prevention tips, and guidance on when to consult a healthcare professional.

Doctor Consultation

Helps users find doctors by specialization, sorted by proximity using geolocation with direct links to view locations on Google Maps.

Responsive Design

Clean, modern, and mobile-responsive UI that works seamlessly across all devices and screen sizes.

How It Works

1

Select Symptoms

Choose all symptoms you are experiencing from our list of 27 predefined symptoms.

2

Get Prediction

Our AI models analyze your symptoms and predict the most likely disease.

3

Get detailed information about treatment, prevention, and when to consult a doctor.

4

Find Doctors
